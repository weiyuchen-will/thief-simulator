# Character face attribution

The face geometry and maps derive from **Infinite, 3D Head Scan by Lee Perry-Smith**, based on work at [triplegangers.com](https://www.triplegangers.com/), originally published by Infinite Realities. Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Source distribution: [three.js / LeePerrySmith](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith). The original `LeePerrySmith_License.txt` is included.

Modifications in this game: scaled and cropped the bust for integration with locally generated bodies, adjusted material/UV settings, added open eyes, role-specific equipment and game animations. Fictional characters use the scan as a common visual base; the depicted missions and roles do not describe the scan creator.

Body geometry, clothing, gear, weapon cosmetics and gameplay are generated locally by this project.
